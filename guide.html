What you need before starting

  • Android phone (Android 8 or newer)
  • GPS turned ON
  • Internet data — about 2 MB per day
  • Your StormLok License Key — format STLK-XXXX-XXXXXX

2
🔑

Install and unlock

The app won't work without this step.

  • Install the StormLok APK on your Android phone.
  • Open the app and tap 🔑 Unlock StormLok.
  • Enter the key exactly as sent — for example STLK-ABCD-123456.
  • Tap Activate.
  • The button turns to ✅ Licensed (green) — you're active.
⚠️
One license = one phone

Your key locks to your device ID (UUID). If you change phones or do a factory reset, re-enter the key. If you see "License mismatch", clear the app data and re-enter your key.

3
🚀

First time start

  • Tap Start Protection.
  • Allow location: choose "Allow all the time" and "Use precise location".
  • Wait about 30 seconds — the app shows your location, for example: "6.1985°, 6.7337° (Asaba)".
  • StormLok fetches CAPE, Rain, Weather Code, and Live Strikes.
  • Status shows ALL CLEAR or STORM RISK BUILDING.
🧠
LIVE ANALYSIS card

Explains the situation in plain English — for example "All clear in Asaba" or "Heads up in Asaba — conditions building".

Choose mode — GPS or Fixed Site

Pick the mode that matches how you use your phone.

GPS Mode

For installers on the move

Tracks you everywhere you go.

  • Best for Asaba, Onitsha, Abuja circuits
  • More battery use
  • Default mode
Fixed Site ✓

Best for home users

Monitors only your house. Saves battery.

  • Settings → tick "Use a fixed site location"
  • Tap 📍 Fill from current position
  • Save — GPS turns off

API keys — optional but recommended

Without keys: StormLok uses weather forecast data (CAPE + storm codes 95/96/99). Still useful, but not live lightning.

With Xweather keys: real live strikes — "Nearest: 8.2 km away".

Daily use

StormLok shows four risk levels. Learn them once, and the app becomes second nature.

Safe
Score 0 – 24
All clear. Solar can stay connected.
Watch
Score 25 – 54
Energy rising. Be ready.
Warn
Score 55 – 94
Storm active. Prepare to disconnect the DC isolator.
Critical
Score 95 – 210
Imminent. Disconnect now.

Keep running in the background

Some Android phones aggressively kill background apps. If yours does, follow the steps for your device below.

⚠️
Tecno, Infinix, and Itel users — this is critical

Without these settings, the alarm may not fire when your screen is off.

Tecno / Infinix / Itel
  1. Settings → Battery → Battery Optimization → StormLok → Don't Optimize
  2. Settings → App Management → Auto Start → StormLok → ON
  3. Recent apps → find StormLok → tap the 🔒 lock icon
Samsung
  1. Settings → Apps → StormLok → Battery → Unrestricted
  2. Settings → Battery → Background usage limits → Never sleeping apps → Add StormLok
Xiaomi / Redmi / POCO
  1. Settings → Apps → Manage apps → StormLok → Autostart ON
  2. Settings → Battery → Battery Saver → StormLok → No restrictions
✅
How to know it's working

After closing the app, you should see a persistent notification: "StormLok Active — Monitoring". If it's there, you're protected.

9
🚨

When the alarm rings

⚠️ StormLok is telling you to act now
  1. Turn OFF the DC Isolator / DC Breaker (the switch between your panels and your inverter).
  2. Tap "I HAVE DISCONNECTED" on your phone — the siren stops.
  3. When the status goes back to ALL CLEAR, reconnect the DC Isolator.
